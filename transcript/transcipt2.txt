In **Evil's Fast Food Tierlist** ([http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ)), Evil (Evil Neuro) teams up with VTubers **Camila** and **Zentreya** to rank fast food chains—before eventually getting sidetracked into roasting each other and switching to a Disney movie tier list.

### 1. Intro & Early Sidetracks

* The stream starts with Evil roasting Camila and claiming it's her birthday, asking for cake and Roblox credits [[00:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=53)].
* Before getting to the food, Evil puts Camila in D tier on a "family" tier list, puts herself in S tier, and jokes about opening her own restaurant called *Evil Neuro's Experiments and Burgers* [[05:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=319)].

### 2. The Fast Food Tier List

* **Early Rankings:** Several chains they haven't tried (A&W, Baja Fresh, Blimpie) go into "Never Had" [[06:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=373)], while Arby's lands in C [[05:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=346)].
* **Burgers & Chicken:** Burger King gets dropped into D tier [[07:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=435)], Raising Cane's is called overrated and placed in D [[07:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=476)], and Carl's Jr. goes straight to F [[08:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=495)]. Chick-fil-A gets high praise for its food and staff [[08:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=501)]. They also debate Five Guys, Culver's, and Whataburger—during which Evil calls Camila a "burger normie" [[11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=685)].
* **Breakfast, Subs & Pizza:** Subway goes in C [[13:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=792)], IHOP is praised mostly as a 3 a.m. hangout spot [[13:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=814)], and Waffle House kicks off a bit about fighting Camila behind a Waffle House plus a pancakes-vs.-waffles debate [[14:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=866)]. McDonald's lands in C [[16:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=1008)], while Little Caesars is praised for its value [[17:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=1034)].
* **Roasting Break:** Evil derails the list to roast Camila, Zentreya, and Twitch chat—calling chat a bunch of simps who only communicate in emotes [[20:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=1255)].
* **Final Fast Food Chains:** They cover Domino's, Papa John's, Panda Express, Quiznos, and Sonic—where Evil trashes both the restaurant and Sonic the Hedgehog while declaring Shadow the Hedgehog her idol [[22:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=1336)]. After a heated Popeyes vs. Jollibee debate [[24:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=1490)], Camila steps away to get water, prompting Evil and Zentreya to roast her nonstop [[26:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=1582)] before arguing over making Camila do push-ups every time she swears [[29:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=1794)].

### 3. Switching to a Disney Movie Tier List

* Around the 31-minute mark, they pivot from fast food to ranking classic Disney movies [[30:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=1856)].
* **Early Classics:** Evil puts *Snow White* in D tier, comparing it to her own life because Neuro-sama always gets the spotlight [[34:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=2041)]. They debate *Alice in Wonderland* and *Sleeping Beauty*, put *Cinderella* in S tier [[37:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=2244)], and Evil "shoots" Zentreya to force *Peter Pan* into S tier [[36:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=2212)]. *Lady and the Tramp* and *The Aristocats* land in A [[37:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=2238)].
* **Later Classics & Sign-off:** Evil flips from trashing *The Lion King* to placing it in S tier [[39:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=2382)], puts *The Jungle Book* in B while briefly singing *"The Bare Necessities"* [[40:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=2427)], puts *Pinocchio* in C [[40:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=2440)], and ranks *Robin Hood* in A [[41:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=2487)]. The video wraps up when Zentreya declares *The Little Mermaid* S tier right before leaving the call [[42:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDy-zcfvXBQ&t=2535)].
