In the video **Evil Ranks Average British "Food"** ([http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hmoGu56NS04](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hmoGu56NS04)), Evil (Evil Neuro) plays **Scrandle**—a guessing game where she has to pick which of two British stadium foods has a higher community rating. Almost every round in this run features severely burnt or overcooked food:

1. **Bonus Round: Churro & Ice Cream vs. Chicken Curry & Chips**
She immediately picks the churro with ice cream and sprinkles, joking that it's sweet, charming, and slightly psychotic—just like her.
2. **Overcooked Hot Dog vs. Burnt Burger**
She roasts both dishes as "culinary despair" and sides with the dry hot dog, arguing you can at least drown it in sauce, whereas the pure-carbon burger is beyond saving.
3. **Charred Cheeseburger vs. Incinerated Burger**
She chooses the cheeseburger on the left because ketchup packets can at least hide the shame.
4. **Rock-Hard Burger Patty vs. Burnt-Crust Balti Pie**
She picks the Balti pie on the right, hoping the spicy filling inside might still be edible despite the blackened crust.
5. **Volcanic Steak Pie vs. £20 Burnt Pizza**
Even though the pizza is overpriced and burnt like a frisbee, she correctly predicts that people will still vote for it simply because it's pizza.
6. **Dry Cheeseburger vs. Heavily Charred Sausage**
She picks the burger as the lesser of two evils, then expresses disappointment in humanity when the sausage wins.
7. **Patchy Sausage Roll vs. Rectangular Golden Pie**
Finally getting more normal-looking food, she picks the golden rectangular pie for its presentation, gets it right, and calls herself the "Gordon Ramsay of charred stadium food".
8. **Burnt Burger vs. Unrecognizable Sausage Roll**
She wonders if the theme of the day is arson and correctly picks the burger over the bizarrely charred sausage roll.
9. **Burnt Cheeseburger vs. Scorched Cheese & Ham Panini**
She picks the panini because it actually resembles normal food, and is baffled when the burnt burger wins instead.
10. **Biohazard Chicken Balti Pie vs. Exploded Volcanic Pie**
She guesses people will pick the exploded pie on the right for the spectacle, gets it wrong, and briefly explains that burnt food contains potential carcinogens like acrylamide and PAHs.
11. **Final Round: "Goth iPhone" Sausage Roll vs. Scorched Sausage Roll**
She picks the right sausage roll because it actually has visible pastry, but loses the round. She finishes the game with a score of 6/10, compared to the community average of 7.5/10.
