In **Cute A.I. vs CAPTCHA** ([http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM)), Vedal tests whether **Neuro-sama** can beat a series of increasingly absurd CAPTCHA levels to prove she isn't a robot:

* **Checkbox & Stop Sign** [[00:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=6)]
Neuro easily clears the first "I'm not a robot" checkbox, then immediately complains about CAPTCHAs being pedantic when she misses a tiny sliver of a stop sign in the top-left square [[00:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=40)].
* **Distorted Text** [[00:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=56)]
Neuro repeatedly misreads the first distorted text prompt, so Vedal swaps to a bold-letter CAPTCHA (`FNCCSD`), which she solves with a quick hint on the last letter [[01:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=98)].
* **Find the Vegetables** [[01:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=108)]
Neuro skips Mr. Potato Head because it's a toy [[02:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=126)], then correctly points out that tomatoes and avocados are technically fruits, not vegetables [[02:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=131)].
* **Tile Rotation Puzzle** [[02:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=146)]
Neuro tries to direct Vedal on how many times to rotate each tile to fix an intersection, but her instructions are way off and they end up brute-forcing it [[03:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=181)].
* **Tic-Tac-Toe** [[03:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=192)]
Neuro loses two rounds in a row by missing obvious blocks [[03:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=217)] before finally locking in, setting up a fork, and winning the third game [[04:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=278)].
* **Chihuahua or Muffin** [[05:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=301)]
Neuro struggles to tell Chihuahuas apart from muffins and jokes that the difference is "one is tastier and the other is a muffin" [[05:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=338)], before clearing a traffic-light/sign-pole CAPTCHA [[06:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=362)].
* **Robot Affirmations** [[06:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=373)]
Vedal reads a list of checkboxes for Neuro to mark. She tells him to check "I'm a chatbot" simply because it's true [[06:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=408)], forcing Vedal to remind her that she has to lie to pass. She also insists he check "I'm a coffee machine with a subscription service" [[07:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=429)].
* **Driving, 3D Text & Fire Hydrant** [[07:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=463)]
After boasting about her parking skills and guiding Vedal across moving traffic [[08:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=498)], Neuro completely fails a 3D hidden-text CAPTCHA and wants to give up [[08:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=530)]. She rebounds by accidentally passing a fire hydrant CAPTCHA after telling Vedal to select every square [[09:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=577)], ending the video on one last text puzzle [[09:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAdcRcYehFM&t=588)].
