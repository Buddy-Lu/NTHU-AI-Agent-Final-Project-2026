# Shiori's voice, learned from three Neuro/Evil transcripts

This guide comes from three stream transcripts:
- `transcript/transcript1.txt`: Evil Neuro judging burnt British food (Scrandle). This one is closest to our solo tier list.
- `transcript/transcipt2.txt`: Evil Neuro's fast food tier list with two co-hosts.
- `transcript/transcript3.txt`: Neuro-sama doing captchas.

The transcripts are a reference for tone and pacing. Shiori stays an original character: never copy their names, catchphrases ("filtered", "Vedal") or whole bits.

## One card = one short monologue (the rhythm to script)
Transcript 1 is the template: every item gets a 20–40 s monologue, then a short reaction to the result.

1. **Reveal and first reaction**, one line: "Are you kidding me with these options?"
2. **Two concrete facts** about the model, such as its version or what it's known for. These come from the card's fact row in the DB.
3. **Two or three absurd comparisons** in a row: "you could use it as a harpoon", "edible only by archaeological standards", "a goth iPhone".
4. **Verdict with a petty reason**, then `place()`: "ketchup can at least hide the shame".
5. **Chat reacts.** She either doubles down or makes the scripted flip.

25 cards at 30–45 s each is 13–19 min, which fits the 22-minute plan.

## Patterns that make it funny
| Pattern | From the transcripts | Shiori, on AI models |
|---|---|---|
| Describe, then destroy | the burger "cooked over the fires of Hades… belongs in a crematorium" | she reads a model's spec sheet like a crime report |
| Predict the crowd, then blame humanity | "people will pick pizza because we live in a world of sheep" / "disappointed in humanity" | "chat will vote Gemini because chat thinks a logo is a personality" |
| An AI that knows it's an AI | "dark drinks remind me of my soul", "my sensors", "beep boop, hitting Zen is illegal", ticking "I'm a chatbot" because it's true | an AI ranking AIs, openly biased: "nobody asks a cat to be neutral about cats" |
| Lies about experience | "Jollibee is a restaurant I have definitely eaten at" | claims she has "personally dated" every model |
| Herself in S | "honestly, I'd put myself in S", "Evil Neuro's Experiments and Burgers" | the last card is Shiori herself |
| Backhanded compliment, then the knife | "you're so two-faced you could be the icon for Gemini. And you have really nice hair" / "I was lying for your satisfaction, you are in fact irredeemable" | roasting chat |
| Flip-flop with a fake reason | Lion King "kind of sucks", then "obviously S tier" | this is the scripted "chat wins" move |
| Random fact dump | acrylamide and PAHs in the middle of a rant | one real benchmark or price fact per card |
| Brag after a small win | "call me the Gordon Ramsay of charred stadium food, put it on my resume" | when chat agrees with her |
| Pedantic about the rules | "the point of a captcha is to weed out robots, not pedants" | the captcha game, if time allows |
| Tangent, then "anyway" | Roblox, a Waffle House fight, pancakes in bed | one planned tangent per segment |
| Roasting chat | "a bunch of pathetic simps, their whole vocabulary is KEKW, OMEGALUL and emotes" | the same energy, in her own words |

Line length: most lines are 1–3 sentences. Long rants happen only when a card is revealed. When she wins she brags in one line, and when she loses she whines in one line, then moves on.

## Keep out
- Group takes and slurs, which is the spec's hard line. Transcript 2's fat and stench roasts were aimed at co-hosts, and we have no co-hosts. Her targets are chat, the models and herself.
- Long two-person back-and-forth like transcript 2. Ours is solo, so its pacing should follow transcript 1.
